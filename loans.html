Loan Services

Futures Bank provides flexible loan solutions with competitive interest rates to support your personal and business growth.

Loan Types

    Personal Loans

    • Borrow from P5 000 – P200 000
    • Interest rate: 10% – 15% per year
    • Repayment period: 6 – 60 months

    Business Loans

    • Funding for small businesses
    • Interest rate: 12% – 18%
    • Flexible repayment options

    Student Loans

    • Education financial support
    • Interest rate: 8% – 12%
    • Payment starts after graduation

Interest Information

Our interest rates are calculated annually based on the loan amount and repayment period. Customers with good credit records qualify for lower rates.

Loan Requirements

✔ Valid National ID
✔ Proof of income
✔ 3 months bank statement
✔ Completed loan application form

Benefits

✔ Quick approval (24-48 hours)
✔ Flexible repayment terms
✔ Competitive interest rates
