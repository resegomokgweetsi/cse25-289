Loan Services

Futures Bank offers flexible and affordable loan solutions designed to help individuals and businesses achieve their financial goals. Our loans come with competitive interest rates and easy repayment plans.

Personal Loans

• Borrow from P5 000 – P200 000
• Interest rate: 10% – 15% per year
• Repayment period: 6 – 60 months

Business Loans

• Funding for small businesses
• Interest rate: 12% – 18%
• Flexible repayment options

Student Loans

• Education financial support
• Interest rate: 8% – 12%
• Payment starts after graduation

Loan Requirements

✔ Valid ID
✔ Proof of income
✔ Bank statements (3 months)
✔ Completed application form
